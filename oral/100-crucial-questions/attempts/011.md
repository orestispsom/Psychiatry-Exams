# The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry — Provisional Attempt 011

English-first draft. Questions only; no model answers.

This iteration preserves the full Adult Psychiatry structure and Greek-viva phrasing of Attempt 010. The 100 main slots are frozen provisionally. The only visible content change is one suicide follow-up added after the 129-item Greek oral-bank mapping audit showed that broad suicide risk factors and disorder-specific patterns deserve explicit rehearsal rather than relying solely on hidden answer coverage.
