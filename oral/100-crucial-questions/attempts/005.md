# The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry — Provisional Attempt 005

English-first draft. Questions only; no model answers.

This iteration is governed by the canonical question-independent `internal/core-coverage.yml` and the question-design doctrine. Main questions test one coherent entity, presentation, treatment, emergency, discrimination problem or bounded foundational concept. Subquestions are used only as genuine examiner pivots; material that simply belongs in a complete answer is not repeated as a visible follow-up.
