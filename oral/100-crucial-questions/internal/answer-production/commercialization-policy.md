# Commercialization Overlay — The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry

Status: governing non-blocking overlay for future commercial use.

## 1. Primary purpose remains exam preparation

The study master is first and foremost a high-quality personal preparation resource for the Greek **Adult Psychiatry specialist-board examination**.

Commercialization must **not** weaken, simplify, delay or distort the study workflow. Research depth, exam-source fidelity, contemporary accuracy and board usefulness remain the governing priorities during study-master production.

The eventual commercial product is a **separate derivative manuscript**, not the private study master exported directly for sale.

## 2. Commercial intent

The completed body of work may later support a premium digital/print educational product for psychiatry trainees and other mental-health professionals preparing for oral/viva examinations or consolidating core clinical psychiatry.

Possible learner-facing features include:

- concise viva answer;
- full model oral answer;
- recall/answer structure;
- likely examiner follow-ups;
- high-yield distinctions;
- common traps;
- contemporary-evidence updates;
- references;
- rapid-review material.

These are product-design options for the later commercial manuscript. They do not change the current study-master requirements.

## 3. Source and expression rule

Authoritative textbooks, diagnostic systems, guidelines, regulatory documents, papers and other sources may be used fully for research and personal study.

The later commercial manuscript must be an **original educational synthesis**. It must not function as a condensed reproduction of Oxford, DSM, Maudsley, Stahl or any other source.

For commercial drafting:

- use original wording, sequencing, explanatory logic and pedagogical structure;
- do not reproduce textbook prose, tables, figures, diagrams, distinctive mnemonics or distinctive source-specific summaries;
- cite sources where appropriate;
- do not reproduce DSM-5-TR criterion text unless the intended use is permitted/cleared; diagnostic requirements should ordinarily be paraphrased and referenced;
- treat ICD-11 material according to the applicable WHO attribution/licensing requirements at publication time;
- factual psychiatric knowledge and original synthesis may be incorporated, but source expression must not be copied;
- short quotations, if ever used, require deliberate editorial/legal review rather than entering by default.

This project file is an internal workflow policy, not legal advice. Before publication, licensing/permission questions that materially affect the manuscript should be checked against the then-current rights-holder terms and, where necessary, professional legal advice.

## 4. Minimal provenance during study-master production

Do **not** add a burdensome copyright review to every question now.

Instead, preserve enough provenance that a later commercial-clearance pass can work efficiently:

1. important claims retain their source references in the canonical dossier;
2. local-source packets record the exact book/edition/section/pages actually consulted;
3. any material that appears unusually dependent on a source's expression or structure is flagged;
4. quotations, copied lists, source-specific mnemonics, proprietary criteria text, tables/figures or distinctive formulations are explicitly marked rather than silently incorporated;
5. final study-master prose should already prefer original synthesis, but commercial clearance is a separate later gate.

## 5. Commercial provenance flags

Dossiers may use the following lightweight flags:

### `expression_origin`

- `ORIGINAL_SYNTHESIS` — independently composed synthesis of supported facts.
- `GENERAL_FACT_PATTERN` — conventional factual/list structure with no meaningful source-specific expression.
- `SOURCE_STRUCTURE_INFLUENCE` — source materially influenced organisation/sequence; later commercial rewrite recommended.
- `CLOSE_PARAPHRASE_RISK` — wording/structure may be too close to a source; commercial rewrite required.
- `DIRECT_QUOTATION` — exact source language is present.
- `PROPRIETARY_OR_LICENSED_TEXT` — DSM criteria, licensed tables/figures or other protected/licensed material requiring special handling.

### `commercial_clearance`

- `NOT_REVIEWED` — default during study-master production.
- `LOW_RISK` — likely reusable after ordinary editorial review.
- `REWRITE_REQUIRED` — knowledge may be retained but expression must be rewritten.
- `PERMISSION_OR_LICENSE_CHECK` — publication depends on applicable permission/licence/attribution terms.
- `EXCLUDE_FROM_COMMERCIAL` — do not carry this material into the commercial derivative.
- `CLEARED_FOR_COMMERCIAL_DRAFT` — passed the later dedicated provenance/copyright review.

These flags **do not block study-master approval**.

## 6. Source-specific safeguards

### Oxford / other commercial textbooks

Use freely for exam-source extraction and private study. For later publication:

- preserve factual conclusions and exam-relevant distinctions through original synthesis;
- do not carry over distinctive prose, diagrams, tables, boxes, mnemonics or chapter-specific summaries;
- page-level provenance is retained internally so later reviewers know what influenced the answer.

### DSM-5-TR

Use as diagnostic authority for study and adjudication. For the commercial manuscript:

- refer to DSM-5-TR and its diagnostic framework;
- normally paraphrase criteria/thresholds rather than reproducing criterion wording;
- flag any exact criterion text as `PROPRIETARY_OR_LICENSED_TEXT` and `PERMISSION_OR_LICENSE_CHECK`.

### ICD-11

Use WHO ICD-11 as diagnostic authority where applicable. Before commercial publication, verify and follow the then-current WHO licensing/attribution requirements for the specific material used.

### Guidelines, papers and prescribing sources

Use factual recommendations/evidence as research inputs, with normal citation/provenance. Avoid reproducing protected tables, algorithms, figures or substantial source wording.

## 7. Writer separation

The study-master writer remains governed by the approved dossier and writes in original language.

The eventual **commercial manuscript writer/editor** should work from:

- cleared/adjudicated knowledge claims;
- study-master answer structure;
- board-fact and examiner-pivot architecture;
- provenance flags;
- a separate commercial style/product specification.

It should **not** simply polish or re-export the study-master file.

## 8. Dedicated commercial transformation stage

Commercial production begins only after the study master is sufficiently mature.

Recommended sequence:

1. freeze a sufficiently complete study-master version;
2. run a dedicated provenance/copyright/licensing audit;
3. identify source-expression risks and proprietary/licensed material;
4. create a clean commercial manuscript from cleared knowledge claims;
5. rewrite organisation/explanation where source structure has influenced the study master too strongly;
6. add original commercial educational features and product design;
7. verify references, attributions and licences;
8. run a final commercial-source-expression audit before publication.

## 9. Non-blocking rule

A question may reach `ANSWER_VERIFIED` and `FROZEN` for the personal study master even if its commercial-provenance flags remain `NOT_REVIEWED`.

Commercial clearance is a **later publication gate**, not a current study gate.

This separation is deliberate: maximize exam-preparation quality now while preserving a clean path to a genuinely original professional educational product later.
