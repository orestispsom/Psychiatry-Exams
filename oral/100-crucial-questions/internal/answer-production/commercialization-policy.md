# Commercialization Overlay — The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry

Status: governing non-blocking overlay for future commercial use.

## 1. Primary purpose remains exam preparation

The study master is first and foremost a high-quality personal preparation resource for the Greek **Adult Psychiatry specialist-board examination**.

Commercialization must not weaken or slow study production. The eventual product is a **separate derivative manuscript**, not the private study file exported directly.

## 2. Commercial intent

The finished body of work may later support a premium digital/print educational product for psychiatry trainees and mental-health professionals preparing for oral/viva examinations or consolidating core clinical psychiatry.

Potential features include:

- concise viva answer;
- full model oral answer;
- recall/answer structure;
- likely examiner follow-ups;
- high-yield distinctions;
- common traps;
- current evidence updates;
- references;
- rapid-review material.

## 3. Source strategy for later commercial use

The commercial product should be an **original clinical synthesis**, not a compressed derivative of any one textbook or classification manual.

During study production:

- Oxford Shorter 2017 may remain the privileged exam-study source;
- publicly referenceable/current authorities such as WHO ICD-11, major guidelines, regulators and primary/review literature should be used where they adequately support current claims;
- DSM-5-TR may be consulted freely for private study and targeted diagnostic cross-checking, but it should **not become the sole or dominant conceptual backbone of the manuscript**;
- final prose should explain syndromes and diagnostic logic in original language rather than reproduce proprietary criterion structures.

For commercial drafting:

- use original wording, sequencing, explanatory logic and pedagogical structure;
- do not reproduce textbook prose, tables, figures, diagrams, distinctive mnemonics or source-specific summaries;
- do not reproduce DSM criterion text unless specifically permitted/cleared;
- treat ICD-11 according to WHO attribution/licensing requirements applicable at publication time;
- cite sources where appropriate;
- use facts/evidence, not protected source expression.

This policy is internal workflow guidance, not legal advice.

## 4. Minimal provenance during study-master production

Do not add a burdensome copyright audit to every question.

Retain only enough provenance to support later transformation:

1. exact Oxford/local pages actually used;
2. current authorities actually checked;
3. flags for direct quotation, close paraphrase, proprietary/licensed text or strong source-structure influence;
4. source identity for important board facts;
5. no need to commercially clear a question before it can be frozen for private study.

## 5. Source-expression risk flags

Use lightweight flags when useful:

- `ORIGINAL_SYNTHESIS`
- `GENERAL_FACT_PATTERN`
- `SOURCE_STRUCTURE_INFLUENCE`
- `CLOSE_PARAPHRASE_RISK`
- `DIRECT_QUOTATION`
- `PROPRIETARY_OR_LICENSED_TEXT`

Commercial clearance states:

- `NOT_REVIEWED`
- `LOW_RISK`
- `REWRITE_REQUIRED`
- `PERMISSION_OR_LICENSE_CHECK`
- `EXCLUDE_FROM_COMMERCIAL`
- `CLEARED_FOR_COMMERCIAL_DRAFT`

These states never block the private study master.

## 6. Source-specific safeguards

### Oxford / other commercial textbooks

Use freely for private study/exam extraction. Later publication should preserve the underlying psychiatric knowledge through original synthesis, not Oxford-specific prose, tables, boxes, diagrams, mnemonics or distinctive chapter summaries.

### DSM-5-TR

Use selectively as a diagnostic authority/cross-check for private study.

For the later commercial manuscript:

- refer to DSM-5-TR where relevant;
- paraphrase diagnostic concepts rather than reproducing criterion wording;
- do not organise the whole product as a DSM-derived criteria digest;
- flag any exact criterion text as proprietary/licensed material requiring permission/licence review.

### ICD-11

WHO ICD-11 is particularly useful as a current international classification reference because it can support a source-diverse, non-DSM-centric clinical synthesis. Before publication, verify and follow the then-current WHO licensing/attribution requirements for the specific material used.

### Guidelines, regulators, papers

Use their factual recommendations/evidence with normal citation/provenance. Do not reproduce protected tables, figures, algorithms or substantial source wording.

## 7. Writer separation

The study-master writer works only from the approved compact answer brief and writes in original language.

The later commercial writer/editor should work from:

- cleared psychiatric knowledge;
- the study-master's original educational architecture;
- source/provenance flags;
- a separate commercial style specification.

It should not simply polish the study-master or preserve the phrasing/structure of a source packet.

## 8. Dedicated commercial transformation stage

After the study master is mature:

1. freeze the study version;
2. run a provenance/copyright/licensing audit;
3. identify source-expression/proprietary risks;
4. create a clean commercial manuscript from cleared knowledge;
5. rewrite any source-influenced organisation/explanation;
6. add original commercial educational features;
7. verify references, attributions and licences;
8. run final publication QA.

## 9. Non-blocking rule

A question may be fully approved for private study while commercial clearance remains `NOT_REVIEWED`.

The study goal comes first; commercial transformation happens later.
