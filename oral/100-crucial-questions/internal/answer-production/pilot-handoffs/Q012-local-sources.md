## Must-cover targets

From Oxford, retrieve enough to support:
- clinical picture: positive, negative and disorganised symptoms;
- hallucinations, delusions/passivity phenomena and formal thought disorder;
- cognitive/affective/catatonic material only to the degree Oxford makes it relevant to the syndrome;
- longitudinal diagnosis and functional/course considerations;
- major diagnostic exclusions/boundaries at overview level;
- Schneider first-rank symptoms and their exam-source framing;
- historical subtype language if Oxford teaches it;
- diagnostic duration/classification points Oxford expects.

From DSM-5-TR, verify only:
- active-phase symptom threshold;
- mandatory core symptom requirement;
- duration requirement;
- functional impairment requirement;
- mood/schizoaffective exclusion;
- substance/medical exclusion;
- developmental/autism clause;
- current subtype/specifier handling relevant to Q12.
