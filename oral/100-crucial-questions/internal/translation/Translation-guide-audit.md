# Translation Guide Audit

## 1. Main problems

The terminology guide provides an initial bilingual mapping across 16 psychopathology domains and an unnumbered appendix. An audit of the document revealed several structural, conceptual, and linguistic deficiencies that undermine its clinical and academic reliability:

1. **Unresolved Contradictions Between Main Tables and Appendix**: The appendix at the end of the document introduces an unformatted bullet list that directly conflicts with the terminology established in sections 5, 11, and 12 (e.g., *Πίεση λόγου* vs. *Λογοδιάρροια* for pressured speech; *Περιστασιακή λεπτολογία* vs. *Περιστασιακότητα*; *Εφαπτομενικός λόγος* vs. *Εφαπτομενικότητα*; *Ανακοπές σκέψης* vs. *Μπλοκάρισμα σκέψης*; *Υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη* vs. *Υπερεγκλειστικότητα*).
2. **Conflation of Distinct Psychopathological Constructs**: Several distinct psychopathological entities are merged under a single entry or translated as synonyms:
   - *Pressured speech*, *Logorrhea*, and *Pressure of speech* are tangled between *Πίεση λόγου*, *Λογόρροια*, and *Λογοδιάρροια*.
   - *Ambitendency* (*Αμφιταλάντευση/Αμφιβουλησία*) is conflated with Bleulerian *Ambivalence* (*Αμφισθένεια/Αμφιθυμία*).
   - *Confabulation* (*Μυθοπλασία*) is conflated with *Pseudomnesia / False Memory* (*Ψευδομνησία*).
   - *Restricted affect* is grossly mistranslated as *Συναισθηματική περίσφιξη* (a surgical/mechanical strangulation term) instead of *Περιορισμένο/συσταλμένο συναίσθημα*.
   - *Overinclusive thinking* (broadening of conceptual boundaries) is mistranslated as *Υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη* (which describes excessive detail/circumstantiality).
   - *Inappropriate affect* (*Απρόσφορο*) is merged with *Incongruent affect* (*Ασύμφωνο*).
   - *Heterodestructive ideation* is equated strictly with *Homicidal ideation* (*Ανθρωποκτονικός ιδεασμός*).
3. **Linguistic and Orthographic Inconsistencies**:
   - Inconsistent register and orthography across sections (e.g., archaic purist *Πτωχεία λόγου* vs. standard modern *Φτώχεια λόγου*; double spelling forms with slashes such as *Ηχολαλία / ηχωλαλία*, *Ηχοπραξία / ηχωπραξία*).
   - Use of colloquial English loanwords or calques (e.g., *Μπλοκάρισμα σκέψης* instead of *Ανακοπή σκέψης*; *Κλαγγικοί συνειρμοί* instead of *Ηχητικοί συνειρμοί / Συνειρμοί κατ' ομοηχία*; *Καλπάζουσες σκέψεις* for racing thoughts; *Ιδιοτροπισμοί* for mannerisms).
4. **Taxonomic Misclassifications**:
   - *Delusional misidentification* (*Παραληρητική παραγνώριση*) is listed under Perception (Section 7) rather than Content of Thought (Section 6).
   - *Body dysmorphia* (*Σωματική δυσμορφία*) is placed under Sleep/Appetite/Somatic Experience (Section 16) rather than Thought Content / OCD-spectrum psychopathology.
   - *Reality testing* (*Έλεγχος πραγματικότητας*) is placed under Judgment (Section 14) rather than as a core ego/cognitive function.
5. **Structural Asymmetry**: The appendix contains entries without English translations (*Νεολογισμός*, *Συμβολική σκέψη*), duplicates main table entries without added value, and lacks section classification.

## 3. Internal contradictions

1. **Pressured Speech vs. Logorrhea vs. Pressure of Speech**:
   - Section 12 lists: `Πίεση λόγου` = `Pressured speech` and `Λογόρροια` = `Logorrhea`.
   - Appendix lists: `Λογοδιάρροια` = `pressure of speech`.
   - *Conflict*: The appendix fuses the two constructs into `Λογοδιάρροια (pressure of speech)`, obliterating the psychopathological distinction between increased verbal quantity/speed (logorrhea) and subjective/objective internal urgency/compulsion to speak (pressured speech).
2. **Circumstantiality**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Περιστασιακή λεπτολογία` = `Circumstantiality`.
   - Appendix lists: `Περιστασιακότητα (circumstantiality)`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 invents a redundant compound term, while the appendix uses the standard abstract noun.
3. **Tangentiality**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Εφαπτομενικός λόγος` = `Tangentiality`.
   - Appendix lists: `Εφαπτομενικότητα (tangentiality)`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 treats tangentiality strictly as a speech phenomenon (*λόγος*), whereas the appendix treats it as a form/flow of thought property (*Εφαπτομενικότητα*).
4. **Thought Blocking**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Ανακοπές σκέψης` = `Thought blocking` (plural, formal Greek).
   - Appendix lists: `Μπλοκάρισμα σκέψης (thought blocking)` (singular, colloquial loanword).
   - *Conflict*: Incompatible stylistic registers and grammatical numbers.
5. **Clang Associations**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Ομιλία κατ’ ομοηχία / ηχητικοί συνειρμοί` = `Clang associations`.
   - Appendix lists: `Κλαγγικοί συνειρμοί (clang associations)`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 provides descriptive Greek phrases, while the appendix imports an awkward literal phonological calque (*Κλαγγικοί*).
6. **Overinclusiveness / Overinclusive Thought**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη` = `Overinclusive thought`.
   - Appendix lists: `Υπερεγκλειστικότητα (overinclusion)`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 mistranslates the concept as "excessively detailed thought" (confusing it with circumstantiality), whereas the appendix provides the correct Cameron construct of broadened conceptual boundaries (*Υπερεγκλειστικότητα*).
7. **Perseveration**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Εμμονή στη σκέψη` = `Perseveration`.
   - Appendix lists: `Επιμονή / Perseveration`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 translates perseveration using *εμμονή* (which typically denotes obsession), while the appendix uses *επιμονή* (persistence) paired with an untranslated English word.
8. **Poverty of Speech (Orthography & Taxonomy)**:
   - Section 12 lists: `Φτώχεια λόγου` = `Poverty of speech` (Modern Greek orthography).
   - Appendix lists: `Πτωχεία λόγου (poverty of speech)` (Purist/Katharevousa orthography) and `Πτωχεία περιεχομένου λόγου (poverty of content)`.
   - Section 5 lists: `Ιδεοπενία / πτωχή σκέψη` = `Poverty of thought`.
   - Section 12 lists: `Αλογία` = `Alogia`.
   - *Conflict*: Inconsistent orthography (*Φτώχεια* vs. *Πτωχεία*) and fragmented classification across Thought, Speech, and negative symptoms without standardized cross-mapping.
9. **Derailment**:
   - Section 5 lists: `Εκτροχιασμός σκέψης / λόγου` = `Derailment`.
   - Appendix lists: `Εκτροχιασμός (derailment)`.
   - *Conflict*: Section 5 conflates thought derailment with speech derailment, while the appendix lists it as an isolated noun.
10. **Echolalia and Echopraxia Orthography**:
    - Section 11 lists: `Ηχολαλία / ηχωλαλία` and `Ηχοπραξία / ηχωπραξία` (offering dual classical/modern spelling variants with slashes).
    - Appendix lists: `Ηχολαλία (echolalia)` and `Ηχοπραξία (echopraxia)` (single standard spelling).
11. **Affect Incongruence vs. Inappropriateness**:
    - Section 9 lists: `Απρόσφορο / ασύμφωνο συναίσθημα` = `Incongruent affect` and `Ασυμφωνία διάθεσης–συναισθήματος` = `Mood–affect incongruence`.
    - *Conflict*: Merges inappropriate affect (*απρόσφορο συναίσθημα*) and mood-incongruent affect (*ασύμφωνο συναίσθημα*) into a single slashed entry, blurring qualitative disharmony with mood divergence.
12. **Format and Structural Contradiction**:
    - Sections 1–16 follow a strict bilingual two-column tabular layout.
    - The terminal appendix abandons the table structure in favor of an unnumbered bulleted list, partially bilingual with parentheses, containing untranslated terms (*Νεολογισμός*, *Συμβολική σκέψη*), untranslated English headers (*Perseveration*), and duplicated entries.
