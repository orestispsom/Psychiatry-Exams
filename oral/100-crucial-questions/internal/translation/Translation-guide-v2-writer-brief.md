# Translation Guide v2 — Approved Writer Brief

Status: approved synthesis of the CLI audit and current terminology research. This file is the sole factual/terminological authority for the Writer rebuilding Translation Guide v2.

## Purpose

Rebuild the existing English ↔ Greek psychiatry terminology guide into a reliable house glossary for:
- Greek Adult Psychiatry board preparation and oral answers;
- psychiatric MSE/clinical documentation;
- translation of *The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry*;
- future psychiatry educational products.

The guide must privilege conceptual fidelity and established Greek psychiatric usage over literal translation.

## Output architecture

Use four columns as the default minimum structure:

`Preferred Greek | English | Accepted alternative / historical term | Usage note`

Do not use slash-separated forms when the slash hides distinct constructs. Use a separate row whenever two English concepts are clinically distinct.

Tag historical/contested items in the usage note rather than presenting them as co-equal preferred terms.

Organize the glossary by clinically coherent domains, broadly preserving the existing MSE structure but moving misclassified terms to their proper domain.

## Global house rules

1. Preserve conceptual distinctions before lexical convenience.
2. Prefer Greek clinical terms used in academic psychiatry over literal calques or phonetic Anglicisms.
3. Use `διάθεση` for mood and `συναίσθημα` / `συναισθηματική έκφραση` for affect.
4. Use `εναισθησία` for insight and `κρίση` for MSE judgment; do not merge either with decision-specific capacity, consent, or reality testing.
5. Use observable phenomenological language when Greek nominal terminology is unstable.
6. Historical but exam-recognisable terms may remain as aliases, clearly labelled.
7. Preserve the English term in parentheses when Greek consensus is genuinely unstable rather than inventing certainty.
8. Do not reproduce the old appendix as a separate conflicting list. Integrate useful entries into the main taxonomy.
9. Canonical orthography for the guide: `πενία`, `ηχολαλία`, `ηχοπραξία`, `ψευδοψευδαίσθηση`; recognised variants may appear once in an alternative-term field.
10. Prefer `άρση αναστολών` in prose for disinhibition; `αποαναστολή` may be indexed as an accepted technical synonym.

## Mandatory terminology decisions

### Speech and thought form

- **Pressured speech / pressure of speech** → `Πίεση λόγου` (preferred); `πίεση στην ομιλία` acceptable. Do not use `λογοδιάρροια` as the canonical translation.
- **Logorrhea** → `Λογόρροια`. Keep separate from pressured speech although they may coexist.
- **Rapid speech** → `Ταχεία ομιλία` / `αυξημένος ρυθμός ομιλίας`. Do not use `ταχυλαλία/ταχυφημία` as generic psychiatric equivalents; those terms belong primarily to speech-pathology/cluttering contexts.
- **Circumstantiality** → `Υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη` / `υπερλεπτομερειακός λόγος` preferred. `Παρεκβατικός λόγος` / `λεπτολογικός λόγος` acceptable. `Περιστασιακότητα` may be retained only as recognition/historical usage. Core distinction: excessive detail but eventual return to the point.
- **Tangentiality** → `Εφαπτομενικότητα` or `εφαπτομενικός λόγος`. The target point is not ultimately reached.
- **Overinclusive thinking** → `Υπερπεριεκτική σκέψη` preferred. `Υπερεγκλειστικότητα` may be recognised as a less natural variant. Never map `υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη` to overinclusive thinking.
- **Derailment** → `Εκτροχιασμός`.
- **Loosening of associations** → `Χαλάρωση συνειρμών` / `χάλαση συνειρμών`. Keep separate from derailment but cross-reference because they overlap phenomenologically.
- **Thought blocking** → `Ανακοπή σκέψης`. Avoid `μπλοκάρισμα σκέψης` as canonical wording.
- **Perseveration** → `Εμμένουσα σκέψη` / `εμμένουσα απάντηση` according to modality. Avoid bare `εμμονή`, which risks confusion with obsession.
- **Poverty of speech** → `Πενία λόγου`.
- **Poverty of content of speech** → `Πενία περιεχομένου του λόγου`.
- **Poverty of thought** → `Πενία σκέψης`.
- **Alogia** → `Αλογία`; keep as a broader negative-symptom construct, not a synonym for every poverty phenomenon.
- **Clang associations** → `Ηχητικοί συνειρμοί` preferred; `συνειρμοί κατ’ ομοηχία` accepted. Avoid `κλαγγικοί συνειρμοί`.
- **Word salad** → `Σαλάτα λέξεων`, cross-referenced to severe `ασυναρτησία`. `Σχιζοφασία` is historical/broader and not an exact synonym.
- **Aprosodia** → `Απροσωδία`.
- **Dysprosodia** → `Δυσπροσωδία`. Keep separate.

### Thought content / thought alienation

- **Thought insertion** → `Παρεμβολή ξένων σκέψεων` / `παρεμβολή σκέψης`. Drop `πρόσδοση` as preferred terminology.
- **Thought withdrawal** → `Απόσυρση της σκέψης`; `υφαρπαγή σκέψης` recognised alternative.
- **Thought broadcasting** → `Εκπομπή της σκέψης` preferred.
- Move **delusional misidentification / παραληρητική παραγνώριση** out of Perception and into Thought Content / Delusional phenomena.

### Memory

- **Confabulation** → `Μυθοπλασία (μνημονική)` as the safest compact glossary form, with a usage note that it is the unwitting filling of memory gaps. Do not equate it with pseudomnesia.
- **Pseudomnesia** → `Ψευδομνησία`; separate construct.
- **Paramnesia** → `Παραμνησία`; broader qualitative distortion of memory.
- **Anterograde amnesia** → house choice `Εμπροσθόδρομη αμνησία`.
- **Retrograde amnesia** → house choice `Οπισθόδρομη αμνησία`.

### Mood and affect

- **Affect** → `Συναίσθημα` for the practical MSE domain; use `συναισθηματική έκφραση` where clarification helps.
- **Restricted affect** → `Περιορισμένο συναίσθημα` preferred. `Περίσφιξη/περιεσφιγμένο συναίσθημα` is attested but should be historical/alternative, not preferred.
- **Blunted affect** → `Αμβλύ συναίσθημα` / `συναισθηματική άμβλυνση`.
- **Flat affect** → `Επίπεδο συναίσθημα` / `συναισθηματική επιπέδωση`.
- **Inappropriate affect** → `Απρόσφορο συναίσθημα`.
- **Mood-incongruent affect/content** → `Ασύμφωνο με τη διάθεση` / `μη σύντονο προς τη διάθεση`, depending context. Do not merge this with inappropriate affect.

### Catatonia / psychomotor

- **Stupor** → `Εμβροντησία` remains board-recognisable and acceptable as the preferred Greek psychiatry term; a parenthetical `κατατονικό stupor` may be used only for recognition when relevant.
- **Catalepsy** → `Καταληψία`.
- **Waxy flexibility** → `Κηρώδης ευκαμψία`.
- **Posturing** → `Κατατονική στάση` / `διατήρηση στάσης` as appropriate.
- **Ambitendency** → no single settled Greek equivalent. House wording: `Κινητική αμφιταλάντευση (ambitendency)`; `αμφιβουλησία/αμφιπραξία` may be indexed as historical/recognition variants. Never equate with `αμφιθυμία` or `αμφισθένεια`, which refer to ambivalence.

### Perception

- **Cenesthetic hallucinations** → `Κοιναισθητικές ψευδαισθήσεις`.
- **Somatic hallucinations** → `Σωματικές ψευδαισθήσεις`.
- **Visceral hallucinations** → `Σπλαγχνικές ψευδαισθήσεις`.
- Keep these distinct.
- **Pseudohallucination** → `Ψευδοψευδαίσθηση`, historical/contested recognition term. Usage note: modern documentation should describe modality, perceived location, reality quality, insight and controllability rather than use this label as a psychosis/non-psychosis boundary.

## Important corrections to the existing guide

The rebuilt guide must explicitly correct these prior mappings/structures:

1. `Λογοδιάρροια = pressure of speech` — remove.
2. `Υπερλεπτομερειακή σκέψη = Overinclusive thought` — replace with the approved overinclusive term; reuse hyper-detail for circumstantiality.
3. `Εμμονή στη σκέψη = Perseveration` — replace with modality-specific `εμμένουσα ...` wording.
4. `Μυθοπλασία / ψευδομνησία = Confabulation` — split into separate entries.
5. `Απρόσφορο / ασύμφωνο συναίσθημα = Incongruent affect` — split.
6. `Αμφιτιμία / αμφισθένεια = Ambitendency` — remove and replace with `κινητική αμφιταλάντευση (ambitendency)` plus historical alternatives.
7. `Απροσωδία / δυσπροσωδία = Dysprosody` — split.
8. `Κοιναισθητικές ψευδαισθήσεις` must not stand in for all somatic/visceral hallucinations.
9. Move delusional misidentification from perception to thought content.
10. Remove the appendix as an independent source of terminology and merge only validated entries into the correct domains.

## Historical / contested handling

Retain only for recognition, clearly labelled:
- `Περιστασιακότητα` for circumstantiality;
- `Περίσφιξη` / `περιεσφιγμένο συναίσθημα` for restricted affect;
- `Σχιζοφασία` as a broader/historical severe-disorganisation term, not a synonym for word salad;
- `Ψευδοψευδαίσθηση` as historical/contested descriptive terminology;
- historical variants for ambitendency.

## Unresolved but non-blocking items

1. **Ambitendency:** no single canonical modern Greek term. Use `κινητική αμφιταλάντευση (ambitendency)` and retain alternative historical variants only in the synonym/usage field.
2. **Confabulation:** `μυθοπλασία` is established but polysemous. Use `μυθοπλασία (μνημονική)` plus an explanatory usage note.
3. **Anterograde amnesia:** multiple Greek variants coexist. House choice is `εμπροσθόδρομη αμνησία`.
4. **Restricted affect:** `περίσφιξη` is genuinely attested, but project preference is `περιορισμένο συναίσθημα`.
5. **Schizophasia:** scope varies; retain only as historical recognition terminology.
6. **Official Greek ICD-11 lexical standardisation:** no comprehensive authoritative Greek CDDR terminology set was identified. Do not overwrite established descriptive psychopathology terms merely to force pseudo-official ICD wording.

## Writer rules

1. Never merge two English constructs merely because Greek clinicians sometimes use one shorthand for both.
2. Use `Πίεση λόγου` for pressured speech and `Λογόρροια` for logorrhea.
3. Use `ταχεία ομιλία` for psychiatric rapid speech; reserve `ταχυλαλία/ταχυφημία` for speech-pathology contexts.
4. Distinguish circumstantiality from tangentiality by whether the endpoint is eventually reached.
5. Keep overinclusive thinking separate from circumstantiality.
6. Keep derailment and loosening of associations as separate but cross-referenced entries.
7. Use `ανακοπή σκέψης` for thought blocking.
8. Do not use bare `εμμονή` for perseveration.
9. Keep poverty of speech, poverty of content, poverty of thought and alogia distinct.
10. Use `ηχητικοί συνειρμοί` for clang associations.
11. Treat word salad as severe incoherence, not a disease entity.
12. Preserve Schneiderian thought-alienation terms as separate phenomena.
13. Keep confabulation, pseudomnesia and paramnesia separate.
14. Use `διάθεση` for mood and `συναίσθημα` / `συναισθηματική έκφραση` for affect.
15. Keep restricted, blunted and flat affect distinct.
16. Keep inappropriate affect separate from mood incongruence.
17. Use `εμβροντησία` as the preferred board term for stupor while allowing recognition aids.
18. Never equate ambitendency with ambivalence.
19. Keep catalepsy, waxy flexibility and posturing distinct.
20. Split aprosodia and dysprosodia.
21. Keep cenesthetic, somatic and visceral hallucinations separate.
22. Treat pseudohallucination as historical/contested and favour phenomenological description.
23. Use `εναισθησία` for insight and describe dimensions when clinically useful.
24. Use `κρίση` for judgment; never use it as a synonym for decision-specific capacity or valid consent.

27. Tag aliases as accepted, historical/exam, contested, or conceptually distinct rather than presenting them as unexplained slash alternatives.
28. Do not add hundreds of new terms. Add only high-yield omissions needed across the 100-question project.
29. Keep the output useful at the point of oral recall: concise terminology notes, not mini textbook definitions.
30. When Greek usage remains unsettled, preserve the English term in parentheses and describe the construct rather than inventing false certainty.
