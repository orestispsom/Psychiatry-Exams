# 1. Executive Assessment

### Manuscript-Wide Strengths
The current manuscript of *The 100 Crucial Questions in Psychiatry* exhibits outstanding structural and pedagogical foundations:
1. **Clinical Organisation & Spoken Flow:** The 2–4 minute oral delivery spine is exceptionally well calibrated across all 100 questions. It prioritises clinical safety, hypothesis-driven exploration, diagnostic formulation, and structured management plans rather than rote checklist recitations.
2. **Contemporary Clinical Logic:** Outdated clinical traps—such as equating psychosis with schizophrenia, using suicidal risk categories for discharge gatekeeping, or treating psychological interventions as chronic-only measures—are consistently and rigorously avoided.
3. **Harmonious Synthesis:** Across diverse subfields (psychosis, mood, neurocognitive, addiction, psychotherapy, and legal frameworks), the model answers demonstrate a mature, patient-centred psychiatric ethos that respects functional recovery, diagnostic fluidity, and statutory boundaries.

### Manuscript-Wide Depth Deficits
Despite these core strengths, the strict conciseness constraints applied during the lean drafting phase resulted in selective manuscript-wide depth deficits that must be rectified before final publication revision:
1. **Pruned Board-Fact Engines:** In several batches (most notably early emergency/assessment questions Q001–Q003, Q005–Q006; foundational psychopharmacology Q079, Q085–Q087, Q089; and psychopathology/neurobiology Q014, Q091, Q094, Q095, Q097), the `Must-know board facts` section was either omitted entirely or compressed into a single line. A board candidate delivering a polished model answer will sound clinically intelligent on first delivery, but may be caught unprepared by the examiner's immediate probing follow-up if core numbers, receptor mechanisms, and criterion thresholds are not explicitly fortified in the revision package.
2. **Asymmetric Question Packages:** Question package Q067 (Sleep Stages and EEG Characteristics) is severely underpowered (only 1,193 bytes), lacking standard quantitative sleep architecture proportions, thalamocortical spindle mechanisms, and full polysomnographic signatures of depression.
3. **Pruned Diagnostic Numerical Anchors & Thresholds:** Discriminating numerical criteria (e.g. DSM-5-TR vs ICD-11 duration boundaries, minimum symptom counts, frequency thresholds for bulimia/binge-eating, and childhood onset age thresholds in ADHD) were sometimes kept purely qualitative in the spoken text without being reinforced in the board-fact block.
4. **Pharmacological Mechanism Precision:** Several major drug classes and specific agents (e.g. mirtazapine, trazodone, atomoxetine, lisdexamfetamine, acamprosate, naltrexone, memantine, AChEIs) are named with clinical indications but lack a dedicated, memorable one-line discriminating receptor/transporter mechanism.
5. **Operational Monitoring & Toxicity Cutoffs:** Therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) levels, haematological stop thresholds, QTc red flags, and lithium toxicity management rules are occasionally qualitative rather than numerically operationalised.

### Highest-Yield Categories of Enrichment
1. **Must-Know Board Facts Expansion:** Populating structured, high-density board-fact blocks across all questions that currently lack them, ensuring every question has retrievable numerical anchors, pharmacological mechanisms, and diagnostic criteria.
2. **Examiner Edge Questions (Probing Follow-Ups):** Providing the exact "Yes, but tell me..." probe that a senior Greek Adult Psychiatry examiner is most likely to ask to test the boundary of the candidate's depth, paired with concise, source-grounded answers.
3. **Discriminating Numerical Structures:** Inserting minimum essential durations, symptom counts, rating scale cutoffs (e.g. BFCRS, CIWA-Ar, 4AT, MoCA, PCL-R, SUSS), and empirical familial recurrence risks without converting answers into unreadable criteria dumps.
4. **Historical Exam Facts Preservation:** Explicitly retaining and labelling classic psychopathology and historical classification concepts (Schneiderian first-rank symptoms, Kraepelinian dementia praecox subtypes, Wernicke overvalued ideas, Jaspers primary delusions, Geschwind syndrome) that Greek examiners continue to probe.
5. **Greek Legal & Statutory Precision:** Anchoring all forensic, consent, and involuntary hospitalisation answers in exact statutory citations (Law 2071/1992, Law 3418/2005, Law 4999/2022, Law 5129/2024, Penal Code Arts. 34, 36, 371).

### Questions Requiring Genuine Correction vs Ordinary Enrichment
The underlying factual accuracy of the manuscript is exceptionally high. No questions contain gross medical errors. However, targeted conceptual/factual adjustments are required in:
- **Q009 (Involuntary Admission):** Ensure the distinction between the legacy statutory text of Law 2071/1992 (which refers to the Πολυμελές Πρωτοδικείο) and the modern procedural reality (Μονομελές Πρωτοδικείο) is explicitly noted, and ensure the 2024 reform (Law 5129/2024) is contextualized as an administrative/service restructuring rather than a change in substantive criteria.
- **Q019 (Clozapine):** Clarify that the mandatory re-titration threshold is >48 hours of treatment interruption, and specify the dual mechanism of CYP1A2 induction by smoking vs downregulation by acute infection/pneumonia.
- **Q040 (Opioid Use Disorder):** Explicitly clarify the pharmacological mechanism of precipitated withdrawal with buprenorphine to prevent dangerous clinical timing errors.
- **Q083 (Lamotrigine):** Ensure the mandatory re-titration rule after >3–5 days (5 half-lives) of treatment interruption is prominently highlighted as a life-saving safety fact.

# 2. Global Revision Rules

The following 18 rules govern the subsequent Writer revision phase:

1. **Spoken Model Core Inviolability:** The spoken Model Oral Answer is already the strongest layer of the manuscript. Do NOT expand the model oral answer with dense tables, long lists of numbers, or encyclopaedic criteria. Recommend `ADD_TO_MODEL_ANSWER` only when an omission undermines the core clinical logic or makes the spoken answer sound evasive.
2. **Must-Know Board Facts as the Primary Factual Engine:** All numerical anchors, receptor affinities, epidemiological figures, monitoring schedules, and classic eponyms must reside in the `## Must-know board facts` section.
3. **Minimum Discriminating Diagnostic Numerical Anchors:** For every major disorder, provide the essential diagnostic numerical scaffold (minimum duration, symptom count threshold, age of onset boundary, and frequency requirement) without dumping DSM-5-TR text.
4. **One-Line Discriminating Receptor/Pharmacological Mechanisms:** Whenever a psychotropic agent is named, provide a precise one-line mechanism (e.g. *Mirtazapine: presynaptic alpha-2 auto- and hetero-receptor antagonist + 5-HT2/5-HT3 and H1 antagonist*).
5. **Stepwise Treatment Rationale:** Clearly state *why* a specific treatment is chosen first-line, why an alternative is avoided, and what triggers escalation to second-line/biological therapies.
6. **Operational Emergency Dosing & Timing:** For psychiatric emergencies (rapid tranquillisation, catatonia, alcohol withdrawal, Wernicke encephalopathy, status epilepticus, TCA overdose), provide exact evidence-based doses, administration routes, reassessment intervals, and maximum daily ceilings supported by existing guidelines.
7. **Operational Monitoring & Red-Flag Action Thresholds:** Replace vague "monitor bloods" statements with exact baseline requirements, maintenance intervals, numerical red-flag cutoffs (e.g. QTc >500 ms, ANC <1000/µL, Lithium >1.2 mmol/L), and the mandatory clinical action.
8. **High-Yield Differential Discriminators:** For every key differential pair, isolate the single most reliable diagnostic discriminator (e.g. ADHD vs Bipolar: chronic developmental trait vs episodic shift; Apathy vs Depression: motivation deficit with emotional indifference vs painful dysphoria/guilt).
9. **Defensible Empirical Risk Anchors:** Include stable, well-established epidemiological risk multiples (e.g. cannabis and psychosis: 2–5x; bipolar I heritability: 70–85%; postpartum psychosis recurrence: 50–60%; suicide risk after attempt: 15–30x).
10. **Explicit Clinical Utility for Biomarkers & Scales:** For every named scale or investigation (e.g. 4AT, CIWA-Ar, MoCA, BFCRS, DaTscan, CSF biomarkers, EEG), explicitly state its specific clinical contribution, sensitivity/specificity context, and diagnostic limitations.
11. **One-Line Psychotherapy Mechanisms:** Whenever a psychotherapy is recommended, state its core active mechanism (e.g. *ERP: inhibitory learning and breaking negative reinforcement loops; DBT: dialectical synthesis of acceptance and change with emotion regulation skills training*).
12. **Historical Exam Facts Preservation & Labeling:** Retain classic eponyms, Schneiderian first-rank symptoms, Kraepelinian subtypes, and traditional psychopathological concepts that Greek examiners expect, explicitly tagging them as `HISTORICAL_EXAM_FACT` to prevent diagnostic confusion with modern ICD-11/DSM-5-TR criteria.
13. **High-Retrieval "Not X → Say Y" Contrasts:** Highlight high-yield misconceptions (e.g. *Not nicotine → tobacco smoke hydrocarbons induce CYP1A2; Not negative tests → positive functional signs diagnose FND; Not diagnosis → court decides criminal responsibility*).
14. **Harmonisation with Current Clinical Practice:** Integrate all verified updates (e.g. ICD-11 dimensional personality model, anti-amyloid antibodies and ARIA, DORAs for insomnia, EIP lower-dose antipsychotic principles).
15. **Deliberate Non-Quantification (`DO_NOT_QUANTIFY`):** Explicitly prohibit adding numbers where estimates are unstable, heterogeneous, or create misleading false precision (e.g. individual lifetime suicide prediction percentages, rigid global violence risk scores).
16. **Genuine Examiner Probing Depth:** Every question package must anticipate and answer the examiner's immediate follow-up probe testing the edge of board knowledge.
17. **Greek Statutory Precision:** Maintain exact citations for Greek legal frameworks (Law 2071/1992, Law 3418/2005, Law 4999/2022, Law 5129/2024, Penal Code Arts. 34, 36, 371), distinguishing Greek statutory reality from imported UK/US legal doctrines.
18. **Strict Derivative and Original Expression Integrity:** Maintain concise, original synthesized phrasing without copying proprietary text from source textbooks.
